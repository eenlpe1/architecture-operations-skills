# Architecture and Operations Skills

Portable decision and operational workflows for engineering agents.

## Language

**Decision skill**:
A workflow that evaluates design choices and records an accepted trade-off. It serves architecture or delivery design rather than immediate incident recovery.

**Operations skill**:
A workflow that investigates current system behavior, coordinates authorized remediation, and verifies the outcome.

**Engagement**:
One scoped operational request, including its investigation, actions, verification, and handoff.

**Case record**:
Private, resumable evidence and action history for an engagement. It is distinct from a public issue about developing the skills.
